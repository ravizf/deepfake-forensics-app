How it works

  • Upload image and normalize size for faster processing.
  • Run visual artifact, frequency, metadata, and classifier checks.
  • Combine signals into prediction, risk score, and heatmap-style evidence.
  • Generate the PDF report only when requested to keep uploads fast.
